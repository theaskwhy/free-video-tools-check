# Free Video Tools Check

"Free" often means watermarked, one-time credits or no commercial use. Check before building a workflow on it. By The Ask Why Company.

## Steps
1. For each candidate tool, look up **today's** official pricing/help pages (free tiers change monthly): credits (one-time or recurring), resolution, **watermark**, **commercial-use rights**, export limits.
2. Classify: **Use** (clean export, commercial OK), **Opener only** (labelled AI clip, short), **Skip** (watermark/no commercial use/no credits).
3. Prefer, in order: real footage (phone, screen recordings, public-domain archives) → free editors with clean export (DaVinci Resolve Free, Meta Edits, CapCut free — verify export) → AI generation only for short labelled openers.
4. AI video included in a subscription the user already pays for (e.g. a Google or other AI plan) may beat standalone free tiers — check what's already owned.
5. Scheduling: use native free schedulers — Meta Business Suite (Instagram + Facebook), TikTok web scheduler (~10 days ahead), YouTube Studio. Third-party schedulers rarely add value at $0.
6. Music: only platform libraries, added inside each app; brand accounts on TikTok use the Commercial Music Library.

## Rules
- Watermarked AI clips look cheap and may be down-ranked; don't post them.
- Label realistic AI content where platforms require it.
- Record the date you checked each tool next to its verdict.
